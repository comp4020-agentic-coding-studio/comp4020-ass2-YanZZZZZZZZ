# Welcome to the masochism club

SLOP3762 · Week 1

---

## The claim

No one signs up to be cheated.

Plenty of people sign up to be **punished**.

---

> The instant after you die, do you have a specific reason to believe the

---

## Two things that look the same

- A boss that one-shots you from full health, telegraphed for a full second
- A boss that one-shots you from full health, with no tell at all

Same death counter. Same damage number. **Not the same design.**

---

## The test

Punishing: the failure is **legible** — you can name the exact decision
that caused it.

Cheating: the failure is **arbitrary** — the numbers were just bigger than
expected.

---

## Punishing case

**Sekiro — Lady Butterfly's double-jump dive.**

Long enough to deflect or dodge. Every death teaches the same lesson,
faster, the second time.

---

## Cheating case

**A damage-sponge enemy.**

No new tell. No new pattern. Just a bigger number between you and the
checkpoint. Failure here teaches nothing.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## The actual product

Not difficulty. **Belief** — conditional on having correctly read what just
happened.

---

## This semester

Twelve weeks, twelve mechanisms for keeping that belief legible: death's
framing, a level's shape, an attack's grammar, a boss's staging, and more.

Every week produces an artefact. Next week: death as a narrative verb.
