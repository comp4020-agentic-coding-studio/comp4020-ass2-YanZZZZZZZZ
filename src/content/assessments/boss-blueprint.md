## The brief

> Design a two-phase boss: the trigger, the narrative justification, and the
> attack phase two introduces.

Build on the boss you started in [Week 2](/sessions/02-risk-ledger/) and
workshopped in [Week 5](/sessions/05-boss-workshop/). A transition earns its
narrative justification before it earns its mechanical one — this brief is
marked on whether that justification is specific to your boss, not a
generic "the boss gets angrier at half health."

## What you submit

A one-to-two page blueprint: the trigger condition, one sentence
justifying it in-world, a description of phase two's new attack and its
telegraph, and one arena or staging change that signals the transition has
happened. Sketches are welcome but not required — the writing has to carry
the argument on its own.

Alongside the blueprint, a 150-word reflection: the moment during drafting
or workshopping where you weren't sure the telegraph was actually
learnable, and what you changed once you noticed. The blueprint shows what
you landed on; the reflection is the only place that shows you noticed the
problem before a playtester had to tell you.

## Where this goes next

The blueprint's boss and its transition logic are fair material for the
[Final Project](/assessments/final-project/)'s opening level — reusing it
there is expected, not penalised.
