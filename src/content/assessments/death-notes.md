## The brief

> Every week, ~200 words on one concrete moment from something you played
> or watched that week — not a review, not a genre generality.

A Death Note is not "Dark Souls' level design is really clever." It's the
specific moment: the shortcut in Undead Burg you didn't realise connected
back to Firelink, the exact frame Lady Butterfly's dive telegraphs on, the
item description that made you distrust your own boss. If the entry could
have been written by someone who hadn't actually played that week, rewrite
it until it couldn't.

## What you submit

Eleven entries, roughly 200 words each, one per teaching week — due at the
start of the session it covers, not held back and bundled at the end. The
best ten of eleven count toward the mark; the eleventh is a genuine free
pass, not something to plan around, because a week you're confident about
still deserves the entry. A late entry scores zero for that week rather
than losing a fraction — see [Policies](/policies/) for what counts as
late and how extensions work.

## Where this goes next

Several of these entries end up being the fastest route into later
briefs — the [Boss Blueprint](/assessments/boss-blueprint/) and
[Loadout Pitch](/assessments/loadout-pitch/) both reward having already
named a specific moment worth building on.
