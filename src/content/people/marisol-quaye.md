Marisol spent six years designing level geography and encounter pacing for
mid-budget action games before moving to teaching, and still keeps a copy
of every studio's internal "why did the playtester quit here" report she
was ever handed. She built this course around a specific frustration: most
design-methodology teaching treats punishing difficulty as an aesthetic
choice rather than a set of decisions that can be named, taught and
reproduced.

She teaches most of the level-design and structural weeks and holds a
weekly office hour for anything about the course's shape — schedule,
marking, or a workshop brief that isn't landing. Consultation hours are
posted on the course calendar and updated at the start of each teaching
block; email for anything that can't wait until the next one.
