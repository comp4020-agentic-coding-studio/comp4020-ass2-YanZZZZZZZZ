Idris came to this course from narrative design — writing item descriptions
and NPC dialogue for a living has a way of making "what does the player
believe happened here" the only question that matters. He marks the
weekly Death Notes and runs the combat, economy and narrative weeks, on the
theory that the same restraint that makes a boss's telegraph fair also
makes a good item description withhold the right amount.

Ask during any session — he's in the room for every workshop, not just the
ones he lectures — or email when a question about a specific submission
can't wait until the next one.
