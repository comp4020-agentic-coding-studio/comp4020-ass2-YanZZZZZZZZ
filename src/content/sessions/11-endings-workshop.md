## Before the session

Bring the boss and world you've been building since [Week
2](/sessions/02-risk-ledger/). If you've changed direction since then,
bring whatever original setting you're using for the [Final
Project](/assessments/final-project/).

## In the session

Write three 50-word endings for that world, in the same unreliable-
testimony voice as your Week 2 item descriptions: a "good" one, a "bad"
one, and a "true" one that isn't just a midpoint between the other two.
None should announce its own category — a reader should have to infer
which is which from tone and consequence alone, the way [weak
guidance](/topics/weak-guidance-and-bad-endings/) argues an NPC should
never announce a destination outright.

We trade endings in the room; if a reader can immediately sort your three
into good/bad/true without hesitation, the ambiguity hasn't been earned
yet.

## Afterwards

Keep the true ending in particular — it's a strong candidate for the
[Final Project](/assessments/final-project/)'s opening script to gesture
toward, even briefly.
