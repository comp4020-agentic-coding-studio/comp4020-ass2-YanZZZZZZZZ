## Before the session

Find footage (or replay, if you own it) of three boss attacks you've died
to more than once, ideally from the boss you started building in
[Week 2](/sessions/02-risk-ledger/). Bring the clips or the timestamps.

## In the session

We step through attacks frame-by-frame on the shared screen. For each one:
where does the telegraph actually resolve — the frame a trained eye could
call the outcome — versus where a player without that training would still
be guessing. Some attacks the room agrees are fair turn out to have very
late tells; some attacks with generous wind-ups turn out to hide a
delayed second hit that breaks the read anyway. Write up the three as an
attack-tells cheatsheet: attack name, the frame it telegraphs on, the
correct response.

## Afterwards

Keep the cheatsheet. Week 5's Boss Blueprint asks for a two-phase boss, and
an attack roster that already has honestly-documented tells is most of
the fairness work done before the transition trigger is even designed.
