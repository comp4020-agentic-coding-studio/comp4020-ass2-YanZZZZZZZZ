## Before the session

Sketch (a paragraph is enough) one original boss: what it was before it
became a boss, and what specifically it lost or misunderstood in becoming
one.

## In the session

Write three item descriptions belonging to that boss — a weapon, a piece of
armour, a ring or trinket — each one testimony rather than a stat block.
The workshop constraint: the narrator telling us about the item has to be
unreliable **for a reason your world supplies**, the way hollowing supplies
Dark Souls' reason. "Cryptic" alone is not the assignment; cryptic-because-
this-specific-character-can-no-longer-trust-their-own-memory is.

We read a few aloud and the room tries to reconstruct what actually
happened to the boss from the three descriptions alone — if they can
reconstruct it too easily, the descriptions were plot summary, not
testimony.

## Afterwards

Keep this boss. Week 5's [Boss Blueprint](/assessments/boss-blueprint/)
workshop builds it into a full two-phase encounter, and a boss whose
belongings already have a voice is most of the narrative work done early.
