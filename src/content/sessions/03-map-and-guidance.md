## Before the session

Pick one area from any Souls-like you've played enough to half-remember.
Don't look anything up.

## In the session

**Part one — the shortcut sketch.** Hand-draw a micro dungeon: a bonfire (or
equivalent safe start), a one-way door or elevator, a boss room, and at
least two shortcut loops connecting them back to the bonfire. Every loop
has to be something a player earns by surviving the long route first — a
loop that's just a second door with no long route behind it isn't a
shortcut, it's a second entrance.

**Part two — the guidance redesign.** Take a screenshot of a visually
cluttered game map (bring one, or use one supplied in the session) and
redesign it using only visual highlighting — brightness, colour contrast,
framing — to draw the eye toward the critical path on first glance, with
no added icon, arrow or text label. If the fix requires a legend, it isn't
a guidance fix.

## Afterwards

Keep both artefacts. They're the direct precedent for the map sketch half
of the [Final Project](/assessments/final-project/) one-pager.
