## Before the session

Think of one stat, in any Souls-like's system, that has a reputation for
being weak or a trap. Bring it, plus a guess at why players call it that.

## In the session

Allocate a 50-point budget to a build centred on that "weak" stat, then
draft the persona it implies: who plays this way, and why, in-world. Write
a first pass at the pitch copy — 150-250 words aimed at a skeptical player
— naming both what the build lets you do that a balanced build can't, and
the specific cost it accepts for that.

We read a few pitches aloud and the room calls out whether the cost named
is real or hand-waved. A pitch that only lists upside hasn't found the
actual trade-off yet.

## Afterwards

Revise into your [Loadout Pitch](/assessments/loadout-pitch/) submission,
due this week.
