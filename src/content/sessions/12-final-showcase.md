## Before the session

Bring a presentable draft of your [Final Project](/assessments/final-project/)
one-pager and your completed [Death Notes](/assessments/death-notes/)
portfolio, ready to submit.

## In the session

Each student presents their opening level in a few minutes: the map
sketch, the two item descriptions, the boss's transition, and the opening
guidance script. Peer review focuses on coherence across the four parts —
does the boss's transition make sense given the item descriptions, does the
opening script's guidance style match the map's own shortcut logic — rather
than critiquing any one part in isolation.

The session closes with the semester's address: not a content recap, but a
statement of what should keep operating in a student's judgment once the
scaffolding of weekly lectures and spec checks is gone.

## Afterwards

Submit the Final Project by its absolute deadline of 2027-05-28 if you need
the extra time past this session to finish the artefact itself — presenting
a draft here and refining it afterward is expected.
