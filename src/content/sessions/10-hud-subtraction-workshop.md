## Before the session

Bring (or use one supplied in the session) a screenshot of a busy MMO or
open-world HUD: quest tracker, minimap, objective markers, buff icons, the
works.

## In the session

Redesign the screenshot by deletion only — no new elements, no
repositioning as a substitute for removal. For every element you cut, write
one sentence naming what the player now has to remember or notice in the
world instead of on the HUD. If you can't name a replacement, the element
wasn't scaffolding a memory task, and cutting it may just be a loss.

## Afterwards

Compare your "at most" HUD against an actual Souls-like's — health,
stamina, equipped item, nothing else running. If yours still tracks an
objective in any form, it hasn't made the subtraction this week's material
argues for.
