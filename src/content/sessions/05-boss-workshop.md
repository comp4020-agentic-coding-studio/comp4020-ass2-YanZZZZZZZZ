## Before the session

Bring the boss from [Week 2](/sessions/02-risk-ledger/) and the
attack-tells cheatsheet from [Week 4](/sessions/04-combat-drill/), if you
built one for the same boss. If not, bring any original boss concept.

## In the session

Design the transition: at what trigger (health threshold, arena event,
an ally's death) does the boss change, and why would *this* boss,
specifically, change that way given what its item descriptions already say
about it. Then design phase two's new attack — telegraphed, per Week 4's
legibility standard — and one staging change to the arena that signals the
transition has happened before the health bar does.

We workshop transitions in pairs: your partner has to guess, from the
trigger and staging alone, what kind of character this boss is. If they
guess something your item descriptions already established, the transition
is doing narrative work. If they guess nothing in particular, it's a
number change wearing a cutscene.

## Afterwards

This is the direct draft of the [Boss Blueprint](/assessments/boss-blueprint/),
due this week — bring the trigger, the new attack, and the justification
sentence, written up.
