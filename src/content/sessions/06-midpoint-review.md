## Before the session

Submit your [Boss Blueprint](/assessments/boss-blueprint/) if you haven't
already. Bring it to the session regardless.

## In the session

No new material this week. We recap the first five weeks as one argument
rather than five separate ones: a level withholds connection, combat
withholds outcome, a boss withholds narrative resolution until it earns a
transition — three subsystems doing the same job of turning restriction
into legible failure.

Then we peer review Boss Blueprints in pairs, checking two things
specifically: does the transition trigger have a justification specific to
that boss, and is the new attack's telegraph actually learnable or just
described as one. The [Final Project](/assessments/final-project/) brief
is released at the end of the session — read it before next week's lecture.

## Afterwards

Bring your partner's feedback into how you revise the boss going forward.
The [Final Project](/assessments/final-project/) explicitly allows reusing
this boss, so a fairness problem caught now is cheaper to fix than one
caught in Week 12.
