## Before the session

If you've played a game with a death-currency mechanic (Elden Ring's
runes, Hollow Knight's geo, roguelike gold-drop-on-death systems), bring
one specific moment where the loss actually landed versus one where it
didn't.

## In the session

Design your own death-currency mechanic: what currency, what's lost on
death, what has to happen for the loss to become permanent, and exactly how
a player recovers it. Then calculate the risk leverage in concrete terms —
what percentage of a player's current wealth is on the line at a typical
death, and how that scales as the number grows across a playthrough. A
mechanic where the leverage never meaningfully changes isn't tuned, it's
decorative.

## Afterwards

Compare your leverage calculation with a partner's. If neither of you can
point to the moment the risk actually changes a decision mid-level, the
mechanic hasn't earned the "economy" name yet.
